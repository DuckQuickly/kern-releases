## Reporting a vulnerability

If you found a security problem in KERN or in its licence server, please email **[SECURITY E-MAIL]**. Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

Include the KERN version, your operating system, what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Proof-of-concept steps help. Please do not include real personal data.

## What to expect

- I acknowledge your report within **5 working days**.
- I assess it and tell you whether I can reproduce it, usually within **14 days**.
- I aim to ship a fix for confirmed issues as a new version and to tell you when it is out. Timing depends on severity.
- I credit you in the release notes if you want, and I will not take action against you for good-faith research that stays within the scope below.

## Scope

In scope: the KERN apps for macOS and Windows, their updater, and the licence server run for KERN.

Out of scope: social engineering, physical attacks, denial of service against hosting providers, and issues in third-party services that KERN only connects to (report those to the provider).

## Supported versions

Only the latest published version receives security fixes. KERN updates itself, so updating is the fix.

## Actively exploited vulnerabilities

If a vulnerability in KERN is being actively exploited, I report it to the competent EU authority as the EU Cyber Resilience Act requires. [Internal: see `internal-records.md`, section "Vulnerability handling".]

## No bounty

There is no paid bug bounty at this time.
