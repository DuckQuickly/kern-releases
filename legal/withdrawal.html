Withdrawal information, model form and checkout texts (English)

(Draft, 7 Oct 2026. Informational translation. The German text is the legally operative one for German-law consumer contracts and must match the statutory model; see widerruf.de.md.)

Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day of conclusion of the contract.

To exercise the right of withdrawal you must inform us ([NAME], [ADDRESS], email: [EMAIL]) of your decision to withdraw by a clear statement (for example a letter sent by post or an email). You may use the model withdrawal form below, but you do not have to.

To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient that you send your communication before the withdrawal period has expired.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay and no later than fourteen days from the day we receive notice of your withdrawal. We use the same means of payment you used for the original transaction unless expressly agreed otherwise. You will not be charged any fees for the reimbursement.

Early expiry of the right of withdrawal

For a contract for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, your right of withdrawal also expires if we have started performance after you expressly agreed that we start before the withdrawal period ends, and you confirmed that you know you lose your right of withdrawal once performance starts.
