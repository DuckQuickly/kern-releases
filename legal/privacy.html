2. In short

  • KERN is local. Your contacts, email content, notes and settings live in a database on your computer. I run no server that receives this content and I cannot access it.
  • No usage analytics and no crash reports. KERN contains no telemetry.
  • I only process: purchase and invoice data, data for the licence check, technical data when you download or check for updates, and messages you send me.
  • Connections you set up yourself (email accounts, AI providers, Todoist, Pushover, calendar) go straight from your computer to those providers. I am not responsible for them, but section 5 explains them.

3. What I process

3.1 Website and download

The download page is hosted on GitHub Pages and installers on GitHub Releases. When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and browser details. The page sets no cookies and uses no analytics. When it loads it asks the GitHub API for the latest version, which sends your IP address to GitHub.

  • Purpose: provide the page and downloads. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (interest in securely delivering the software).
  • Recipient: GitHub Inc. / Microsoft (USA). See sections 6 and 7.

3.5 Communication and support

If you write to me, I process your message and contact details to reply. If you send a support bundle from KERN, it contains technical information about your installation. It contains: app version and build, operating system, the install folder path (with the user-name part removed), the email addresses of your own connected mailboxes, counts (for example number of contacts), notification and calendar-ID settings, licence status, and which stages of recent runs reported problems (category only; the error texts are removed). It does not contain contact records, email content, private notes, API keys or sign-in tokens. Please review it before sending.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. Retention: until the request is resolved, then at most [12] months unless a legal duty requires longer.

4. What stays on your device

KERN stores locally: contacts, aliases, email history and content (depending on your settings), notes, application and event data, tasks, AI cache, credentials (in the macOS Keychain or the Windows credential store) and settings. This data leaves your device only through connections you enable (section 5), or when you copy, export or back up data elsewhere. KERN offers export, deletion of individual items, and complete removal of local data.

5. Connections you set up

You decide the purpose and means for this processing and you contract with the providers. I do not receive the data.
